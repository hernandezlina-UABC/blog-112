About Me

Hi, I'm Lina (26). My absolute favorite thing in the world is snowboarding while listening to music and admiring the scenery. Half of it is pure adrenaline—just trying not to die—and the other half is refueling with enough energy and motivation to survive adulting. Lately, I've also found a lot of joy in some really good books, though I definitely don't read enough to call it a hobby. Long story short: I just miss the snow so bad.
